Employee Separation Procedure

Document ID: CNT-HR-023

Version: 1.0

Effective: January 1, 2026

Notice

Employees resigning should provide notice consistent with their employment agreement and applicable law.

Company property

Company laptops, badges, keys, and other assets must be returned by the employee's final working day unless People Operations provides different instructions.

Access

IT disables company system access according to the separation schedule.

Benefits

People Operations provides information about benefit termination and any available continuation options.

Final expenses

Outstanding business expenses should be submitted through the expense system before separation whenever practicable.
