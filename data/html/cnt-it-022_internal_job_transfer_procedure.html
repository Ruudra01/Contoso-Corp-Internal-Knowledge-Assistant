Version: 1.0

Effective: January 1, 2026

Eligibility

Regular employees may apply for internal positions after completing six months in their current role, unless Talent Acquisition approves an exception.

Application

Employees apply through the internal careers portal. The current manager is notified when the candidate reaches the interview stage.

Selection

Selection is based on qualifications, demonstrated performance, role requirements, and business need.

Transfer timing

Selected employees normally transfer within 30 calendar days after final approval, subject to business needs and an agreed transition plan.
