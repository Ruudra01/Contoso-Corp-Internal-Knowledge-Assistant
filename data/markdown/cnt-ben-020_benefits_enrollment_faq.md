# Benefits Enrollment FAQ

**Document ID:** CNT-BEN-020  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** January 1, 2026

## When must I enroll?

New hires must complete benefit elections within **30 calendar days of their employment start date**.

## What if I have a qualifying life event?

Certain events may permit a mid-year election change. Contact People Operations promptly because plan rules can impose a 30-day submission window.

## Who is eligible for health coverage?

Full-time employees scheduled for at least 30 hours per week are eligible under CNT-BEN-004.

## Which document controls?

The applicable insurance plan documents and Summary Plan Description control over this FAQ if there is a conflict.
