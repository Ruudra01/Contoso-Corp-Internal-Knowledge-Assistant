# PTO Request Procedure

**Document ID:** CNT-HR-019  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Standard requests

Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR system before the leave begins whenever reasonably possible.

Requests of **three or fewer consecutive business days** require manager approval.

Requests of **more than three consecutive business days** require manager and People Operations approval.

## Emergencies

If an emergency prevents advance submission, notify the manager as soon as practicable and submit the request when able.

## Important distinction

The number of PTO days an employee receives is governed by CNT-HR-005 and CNT-HR-011. This procedure defines approval workflow, not the annual PTO entitlement.
