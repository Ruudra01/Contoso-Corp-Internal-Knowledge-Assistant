# IT Equipment & Asset Policy

**Document ID:** CNT-IT-024  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** January 1, 2026

## Company equipment

Contoso provides equipment appropriate for the employee's role. Company laptops must use approved security controls and management software.

## Acceptable use

Company equipment is primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted when it does not interfere with work, violate policy, create security risk, or incur material cost.

## Loss or theft

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security immediately.

## Remote work

Employees working remotely must protect company equipment from unauthorized access and must not leave company devices unattended in insecure public locations.

## Return

All company equipment must be returned when employment ends or when IT requests replacement or recovery.
